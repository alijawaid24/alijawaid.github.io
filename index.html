Introduction

About Ali Jawaid

A detailed overview of academic development, technical learning, professional responsibilities and space-science interests.

Ali Jawaid is a Space Science and Technology student at the University of Karachi, majoring in Aerospace Engineering. His academic interests include aerospace systems, space exploration, satellite technology, astronomy, Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and remote sensing.

Alongside his studies, Ali has developed practical experience in industrial plant operations through a one-year apprenticeship at National Foods Company Limited, Port Qasim Plant. During this programme, he gained exposure to production-line management, quality assurance, industrial maintenance, stock control and traceability systems. His résumé records an “Excellent” performance rating, completion of a 24-month curriculum in 12 months, and 98% attendance with zero safety incidents.

He also served for four years as a Team Leader in the Expanded Programme on Immunization (EPI) Polio Campaign in Karachi. This role involved supervising field teams, coordinating vaccination targets and area coverage, mentoring team members, maintaining records and working with communities and health supervisors.

To broaden his technical knowledge, Ali has completed structured learning in remote sensing, GIS-based modelling, Earth observation concepts, radio astronomy, satellite technology, space robotics and German language studies. He also registered for and remotely attended the 2026 Sagan Summer Workshop hosted by the NASA Exoplanet Science Institute at Caltech. His current interests include satellite applications, planetary and exoplanet science, geospatial analysis and the use of Earth-observation data to understand environmental processes. The certificates and workshop records below document learning and participation; they should not be read as claims of professional certification or independent research experience beyond what each record states.
